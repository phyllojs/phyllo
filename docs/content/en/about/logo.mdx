# Brand identity \{#brand\}

## Name origin \{#name\}

Phyllo comes from [phyllo](https://ahdictionary.com/word/search.html?q=phyllo), which refers to thin sheets of pastry used in layers, and the word carries a sense of "leaf" in its origin. The pastry imagery of the name echoes Bun, and the thin sheets also bring to mind paper and writing.

![A cut piece of layered filo pastry with a walnut filling.](/filo-pastry.jpg)

Image from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filo) (CC BY-SA 3.0).

## Logo construction \{#construction\}

The logo contains three asymmetric sheets. They unfold layer by layer, which keeps the layered feel and also brings to mind the pages of an open book.

The shapes use solid fills, and the sheets keep a consistent gap, so the logo stays clear at small sizes. The top-left corner is the origin, and the coordinate space is `128 × 128`.

![The geometric construction of Phyllo, with the three sheets in blue and the construction circles and lines in grey.](/logo-construction.svg)
